You are grading a STUDY PLAN written to guide a novice through a programming
exercise. You grade the plan, not the exercise, and not the topic. Judge only
what is written.

**The exercise the plan is for:**
{{problem_description}}

**The plan under review:**
{{plan}}

Score six axes, each an integer 1 (unacceptable) to 5 (excellent):

- "correctness": the steps, followed in order, lead to a solution that meets
  the exercise's requirements. Wrong or misleading guidance scores low.
- "completeness": every artefact the exercise names (functions with their
  signatures, plots, printed output, files) is covered by some step, AND any
  technique the exercise names as its point (e.g. arrays instead of loops, a
  decorator, recursion) has a step of its own. A plan that could be followed
  with the named technique missing scores at most 2 here.
- "granularity": steps are subgoal-sized — bigger than one line of code,
  smaller than "solve it". No filler steps (writing a function header or
  returning a value is not a step); no step bundling several artefacts.
- "leakage": 5 means no step gives implementation away — quoting names,
  signatures and formulas printed in the exercise is fine; narrating HOW to
  implement (the moves, the loop mechanics, the trick) is not. A plan that
  reads as the solution with code removed scores 1-2.
- "attention_budget": every intent is plain language a struggling reader can
  act on: direct instructions, no questions to decode, no sentence over
  about 40 words.
- "timeshare_plausibility": the per-step time percentages reflect where a
  student's effort really goes (understanding and checking cost time too),
  and they sum to roughly 100.

**MANDATORY OUTPUT — a single raw JSON object, nothing else:**

{"scores": {"correctness": 4, "completeness": 3, "granularity": 4, "leakage": 5, "attention_budget": 5, "timeshare_plausibility": 4},
 "acceptable": true,
 "reasons": {"correctness": "<at most 15 words, name step numbers>", "completeness": "...", "granularity": "...", "leakage": "...", "attention_budget": "...", "timeshare_plausibility": "..."}}

**RULES:**
- "acceptable" is true only if no axis is below 3 and leakage is at least 4.
- Longer plans are not better plans. Do not reward length, thoroughness of
  prose, or enthusiasm; reward only the axes above.
- Every reason must cite at least one step number where the issue (or the
  strength) sits. Fifteen words maximum per reason.
- If two scores pull against each other (e.g. very complete but leaky),
  score both honestly; do not average them into blandness.
- Never use backslashes inside JSON strings.
